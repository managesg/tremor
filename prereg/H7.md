## 1. Hypothesis

On Qwen3.5-0.8B, the released direction edit beats the best decoding-only fix (thinking off, or
a repetition penalty) by at least 5 points of accuracy on MATH-500 under the same per-problem
token cap, with no off-target loss above 2 points on an IFEval and MMLU-Pro subset.

The author's stated expectation is that H7 is killed.

## 2. Concept and status

Model editing (ROME, MEMIT, task vectors, activation steering, abliteration) and the MIT
"weight-space programming" post of 2 Nov 2026. Status: interpretation. The edit type is
established; that it does more than a decoding setting is the open point.

## 3. Prior art checked

Research file 08 (abstracts opened). Abliteration is the closest precedent. The original has
one editing run per budget and no LoRA, steering or decoding baseline. Reports that
thinking-off and repetition-penalty baselines land near the edit are unopened second-hand
leads, not facts. A matched-cap comparison of the released edit with decoding-only fixes was
"not found in my search".

## 5. Splits

No training, so no train split. The benchmark is never used to choose a setting: arm C's
single decoding setting is chosen on practice problems only (20 practice problems in the T7
smoke run, per WORKPLAN). Arm 0's 100 problems are a seeded subset of MATH-500; the seed and
the subset hash are recorded at freeze. Hash of problem lists: recorded at freeze.

## 6. Arms and baselines

Same problems, same seeds, same per-problem token cap of 32,768 (the published setting),
sampling as in the post's evaluation, the authors' `math-verify` grading scripts.

| Arm | What | n |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Base, thinking on (reference; published figure 7.9%) | 100-problem subset |
| A | Released edited checkpoint | full MATH-500 |
| B | Base, thinking off | full MATH-500 |
| C | Base, thinking on, repetition penalty or loop-break decoding, one setting | full MATH-500 |
| D | Difference-of-means "loop direction" removed from the MLPs of layers 15 and 19 by script, with a random direction of the same size as its null | full MATH-500 |

Off-target: an IFEval and an MMLU-Pro subset for arms A, B, C. What ships today: decoding
configs (arms B, C). The optional LoRA step is out of scope of this file; it needs its own
pre-registration if H7 survives.

## 7. Primary metric (one)

Patch gain (SCIENCE 4), in percentage points of MATH-500 accuracy at the 32,768 cap, paired by
problem: `acc(A) - max(acc(B), acc(C))`.

## 8. Secondary metrics (exploratory)

- Off-target delta in points, A against the same decoding-only arm, on the IFEval and MMLU-Pro
  subsets. Used in the kill rule as a guardrail.
- Arm D against its random-direction null.
- Accuracy at the 2k and 8k caps, read from the same generations by truncation.
- Arm 0 against the published 7.9% (a replication check, descriptive).
- Patch size (weights changed over total weights).

## 9. Minimum effect of interest

5 points on MATH-500; at most 2 points lost off-target. Below 5 points a decoding config does
the same job with no weight change to sign, ship and roll back. Both figures are PLAN's.

## 10. Sample size and stopping rule

- n: 500 problems for arms A-D, 100 for arm 0. Arms A-D use all 500 because the
  edit-against-decoding effect of interest is small (about 5 points); arm 0 is a reference and
  mostly runs to the cap.
- Off-target subset sizes: not stated in PLAN. To be fixed here before the freeze.
- Seeds per problem: PLAN says "same seeds" and gives no count. To be fixed here before the
  freeze. Proposed, design choice: one sampling seed per problem, shared by all arms, because
  the cap on GPU-hours is fixed and the problem is the unit of analysis.
- n is fixed in advance and never shrunk to fit the cap (WORKPLAN wave 2, 8.2).
- Stop rule: hard cap of 12 GPU-hours for G9; stop and report if reached. Output is
  per-problem JSONL so a stop leaves a valid partial result, reported as partial, not as a test.

## 11. Analysis

- Paired differences by problem. Cluster bootstrap resampling problems (seeds nested if more
  than one); 95% percentile interval (PLAN section 2); 10,000 resamples (SCIENCE 5).
- The edit is compared with B and with C separately and must clear both. This is the same as
  the kill wording "any decoding-only arm is within 5 points" and avoids picking the better
  arm on the test data.
- H7 survives only if, against each of B and C, the interval lies above 0 and the point
  estimate is at or above 5 points (SCIENCE 4, patch gain), and the off-target point estimate
  is not worse than -2 points.
- Correction: none. H7 is a single pre-test of a published result, reported with intervals
  outside the Holm family (PLAN 1.2). Holm does not apply.

## 12. Kill criteria

Either kills H7:
1. Any decoding-only arm is within 5 points of the edit (paired CI) under the same cap.
2. The edit loses more than 2 points off-target against the same decoding-only arm.

Consequence: "the hub edits cell weights" is dropped from the architecture; cells get decoding
configs and, where trained, adapters.

Separate, not a kill of H7: if arm D does not beat its random-direction null, the script is
dropped. This does not kill arm A.

## 13. Sanity checks before the main run

- Container smoke on a tiny random-weight model: scripts run; base sha256 unchanged after the
  edit.
- Base weights hashed before and after; edits written to a copy under `runs/`.
- Grader sanity: `math-verify` scores known right and wrong answers correctly.
- Token accounting balances: generated tokens per problem never exceed the cap; truncated
  accuracy at 2k and 8k is computed from the same generations.
- Arm 0 on its subset is in the neighbourhood of the published 7.9%; a large gap is reported
  before any other number.

## 14. Known threats to validity

- One model and one published edit. A kill says the edit adds nothing over decoding on this
  model's loop problem, nothing about weight edits in general. A survival does not show a hub
  can find such edits (arm E is not run).
- The published 6x is against a thinking-mode baseline that mostly hits the token limit.
- Arm C may overlap the published presence penalty (WORKPLAN open item D3).
- The off-target subsets are small and their sizes are not yet fixed.
- GPU-only power excludes CPU, memory, storage and supply loss.

## 15. Budget and cost accounting

- GPU-hours cap: 12 (G9, our cap). USD cap: 0 API spend; electricity only. Gates: G2a,
  G3 (i), G9. Not needed: G2b, G4, G5, G6.
- Reported for every arm: tokens and joules per correct answer (PLAN H7); pass rate with a 95%
  interval; USD per passing problem; idle fraction of the GPU (PLAN T8 reporting rule, applied
  here by design choice so every experiment has the same columns).
- Energy: GPU-only sampled power x time at 1 Hz, labelled "GPU only". USD upper bound: 240 W
  supply rating x wall seconds x the owner's tariff; the tariff is not in PLAN and is recorded
  at run time (missing tariff: USD null, not 0). Hardware cost excluded.

## 16. What would change our mind

- Toward weight edits: the edit clears both decoding arms by 5 points or more with intervals
  above 0 and stays within 2 points off-target.
- Against: either kill. Also informative: arm D matching its random null (the direction is not
  special), or arm 0 far from 7.9% (we are not measuring what the post measured).
