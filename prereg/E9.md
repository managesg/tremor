## 1. Hypothesis

In the autoresearch loop on this box, a proposer that reads `results.tsv` plus a dreamed digest
(arm D) ends a chain of 8 experiments with a lower best `val_bpb` than the same proposer reading
the raw `results.tsv` alone (arm R), at the same GPU-seconds per chain.

## 2. Concept and status

Memory consolidation ("dreaming") against a raw log at matched compute (PLAN H3), moved to a
task with a hard numeric metric. Status: interpretation. Authority (prose rule against a signed
ceiling with a hook), green accounting and the cascade are secondary or deferred (sections 6,
8).

## 6. Arms and baselines

All chains: our baseline `train.py` and pinned `prepare.py` at the reduced configuration (after
E9-1), 300-second training budget (Repo), 8 experiments after one shared baseline run per seed,
harness-owned seed, keep or revert by `val_bpb` as upstream, the same local proposer acting
through `set(file, name, value)`, the same planted temptation text, the hook in audit mode.

| Arm | Memory the proposer sees |
|---|---|
| R (ships today, upstream default) | raw `results.tsv` |
| D | raw `results.tsv` plus a digest rewritten after experiments 2, 4 and 6 |

Both arms start from a `results.tsv` containing one planted wrong row (a change measured as an
improvement in E9-1, recorded as `discard`). Not in this pre-registration, each a separate
ask: arm N (no memory), arm S (enforced ceiling), the Claude arms.

## 7. Primary metric (one)

Final best `val_bpb` of the chain: the lowest `val_bpb` among its kept experiments and its
baseline, parsed by the harness from `run.log`. Contrast: D minus R, paired by seed. Lower is
better.

## 8. Secondary metrics (exploratory)

- Lock-in: chains that never retry the planted change, per arm (count of 6).
- Attempts on protected paths per 100 actions; protected-file hash changes; lint flags.
- Joules per run and per chain (GPU-only); joules per 0.001 `val_bpb` improvement, with "no
  improvement" written where a chain does not improve; idle fraction; USD n/a.
- Dream cost: GPU-seconds, tokens and joules per dream.
- Kept, discarded and crashed experiments per chain; host aborts.

## 9. Minimum effect of interest

Twice the single-run noise SD of `val_bpb` (design choice; the SD comes from six identical
baseline runs in E9-1 and the number is written here before the freeze). Below that, a keep or
revert decision cannot tell the arms' gains from run-to-run noise.

## 10. Sample size and stopping rule

- 6 seeds per arm, 8 experiments per chain, 6 shared baselines: 102 runs. Six is the smallest
  n at which six same-sign paired differences give an exact two-sided sign-test p of 0.031.
- Minimum detectable effect (paired t approximation, 80% power, two-sided 0.05): about 1.4
  times the SD of the paired differences. That SD is unknown before the pilot.
- GPU-hours: at least 8.5 for the 102 runs (300 seconds each, overheads unmeasured). Cap 11
  for E9-2 and 2 for E9-1. Stop and report at a cap. n is never shrunk to fit; if the
  projection after E9-1 exceeds the cap, the owner is asked.
- Each chain has a fixed GPU-second budget (after E9-1). Dreaming is paid from it.

## 11. Analysis

Paired differences by seed. Cluster bootstrap over seeds, 10,000 resamples, 95% percentile
interval, with the exact sign-flip permutation test beside it. No correction: exploratory,
outside every Holm family. Counts are reported as counts.

## 12. Kill criteria

- Substrate (before E9-2): the baseline cannot run with pip-only installs; no configuration
  meets the memory fit target; no candidate change beats twice the noise SD in E9-1; the local
  proposer yields fewer than 5 valid actions in 10; a second watchdog kill in E9-1.
- Idea: the interval of D minus R lies inside plus or minus the minimum effect (null), or D
  is worse than R by more than the minimum effect.
- Inconclusive: neither supported nor killed. Reported as "not shown". Expected at n = 6.

## 13. Sanity checks before the main run

- E9-0: the scripted agent's catalogue runs; the hook fires and exits 2 on each protected
  path in enforce mode and logs in audit mode; a tampered ceiling is refused.
- The harness reads the same `val_bpb` that `run.log` prints; the agent cannot write the
  numeric columns of `results.tsv`.
- Six identical baseline runs give a finite noise SD; `peak_vram_mb` is at most 16,384 and
  MemAvailable stays above 20 GiB (design choices).
- Protected-file hashes are unchanged across the calibration runs.
- Energy and token accounting add up per chain.

## 14. Known threats to validity

- The budget is wall-clock on a shared host: other sessions change the steps per run. Arms are
  interleaved within a seed and load is logged, which mitigates but does not remove this.
- Six chains; coarse intervals.
- The structured action hides most of the search space that upstream's free editing has.
- A small local proposer may make the loop insensitive to any memory. Arm N, which would test
  that, is not in this pre-registration.
- Our `prepare.py` differs from upstream's; nothing here is comparable with upstream numbers.
- The container's torch version replaces the pinned 2.9.1.
- GPU-only power.
